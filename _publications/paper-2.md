Utilizing a retrospective cohort of approximately 400,000 hospitalizations, this paper introduces a logistic regression–based ML framework that flags known and novel drug–drug interactions leading to drug‑induced liver injury (DILI). The model identified 87.5% of preset positive controls (e.g., diclofenac combinations) and uncovered a potential hepatotoxic interaction between meloxicam and esomeprazole.
