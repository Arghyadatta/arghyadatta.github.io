An interpretable machine learning approach was developed to assess and reduce the risk of hospital-acquired venous thromboembolism (VTE) through the administration of ondansetron. This work demonstrates a move away from opaque “black box” models by using conversational (interpretable) ML to provide actionable insights to clinicians. As a result, the model not only identifies risk patterns but directly informs interventions that reduce VTE incidence. Follow‑up work will expand this approach to other commonly administered medications and conditions.
