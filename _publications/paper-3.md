This work delves into the biochemical mechanisms underlying thiazole ring bioactivation by contrasting meloxicam (bearing a methyl group) with sudoxicam. The study reveals that the presence of the methyl substituent modulates enzyme specificity and the resulting bioactivation pathway—insights that have direct implications for drug safety and design. Follow-up research could explore structural modifications to further mitigate toxicity.
