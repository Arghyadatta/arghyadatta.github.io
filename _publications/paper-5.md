Cal‑net is a neural architecture designed to tackle imbalanced binary classification by simultaneously optimizing for accuracy and well-calibrated probabilities. By incorporating a dual-objective loss function, the model achieves strong predictive performance while maintaining trustworthy confidence estimates—particularly useful in high-stakes decision-making. Follow-up work may include extensions to multi-class settings and real-world calibration benchmarks.
