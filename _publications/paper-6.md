This paper examines **prediction instability** in DNNs caused by training stochasticity, introducing per-sample label-entropy metrics to identify unstable inputs. The authors propose a data-centric regularization method called Temporal Guided Temperature Scaled Smoothing (TGTSS), achieving up to 90% of ensemble-level stability at far less cost. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
