This work introduces the **wave** architecture, a neural model that propagates information across undirected graphs in “waves” to capture long-range dependencies more effectively than traditional graph convolution. The model was evaluated on tasks like path labeling, maze-solving, and circuit voltage prediction—scaling from small to large graphs with improved accuracy and efficiency. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
