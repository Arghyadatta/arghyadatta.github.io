Fair‑Net presents a neural design aimed at minimizing performance disparities across known sub-populations. By incorporating fairness-aware loss functions and representation balancing within the architecture, the model demonstrates reduced accuracy gaps on benchmark datasets. Future extensions include bias auditing tools and intersectional fairness evaluation.
