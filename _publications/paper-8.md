SOP‑Bench introduces a synthetic data framework for generating realistic, industrial-grade Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) aimed at evaluating planning, reasoning, and tool-use abilities of LLM-based agents. The benchmark comprises over 1,800 diverse tasks spanning 10 industrial domains, complete with APIs, tool interfaces, and human-validated test cases. Evaluations of two leading agent architectures—Function-Calling and ReAct—reveal modest average success rates of 27% and 48%, respectively, with nearly universal tool mis-invocation when faced with overly large tool registries. These results spotlight a significant capability gap in current agents for automating complex, structured workflows. SOP‑Bench and its prompt-generation framework are publicly released to support further development and domain-specific extensions in industrial automation research
