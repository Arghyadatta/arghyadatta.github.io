During the Spring 2019 semester, I taught **Intro to Machine Learning (CSE 417T)** at Washington University in St. Louis—the course had the **largest enrollment on record till then**, with approximately **170 undergraduate students**.

- Led **hands-on labs**, guiding students through real-world supervised learning techniques using Python and MATLAB.
- Evaluated **homework, project submissions, and exam papers**, providing detailed feedback to help learners master concepts such as linear/logistic regression, neural networks, kernel methods, decision trees, and ensemble models.
- Supported discussions on **fairness, accountability, transparency**, and ethics in machine learning, and helped students understand model generalization, overfitting, and regularization :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}.
- Course materials, assignment frameworks, and labs were hosted alongside the official course on GitHub, ensuring reproducibility and collaborative version control.

For more information, see the official course listing: https://courses.wustl.edu/CourseInfo.aspx?sch=E&dept=E81&crs=417T
